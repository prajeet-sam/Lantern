# 🏮 Lantern

> A private, gentle companion for understanding your cycle, noticing patterns, and preparing for what's ahead.

🔗 **Live App:** [lanternforher.netlify.app](https://lanternforher.netlify.app/)

---

## About

Lantern is a cycle-tracking wellness companion built around **identity and self-understanding — not shame**.

Most period/cycle trackers lean on streaks, guilt-driven notifications, and calorie counting to drive engagement. Lantern intentionally avoids all of that. It's designed to feel less like a tracking tool you're obligated to open every day, and more like a quiet, private companion that helps you notice patterns in your body and feel prepared for what's coming — without judgment, pressure, or gamified guilt.

The goal isn't behavior modification through anxiety. It's building a calmer, more informed relationship with your own cycle.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Private cycle tracking** — log and view your cycle with no public profiles, social feeds, or comparison mechanics
- **Pattern recognition** — surfaces trends over time so you can understand your body rather than just log data
- **Predictive insights** — helps you anticipate upcoming phases so you're prepared, not caught off guard
- **No shame-based mechanics** — no streaks to break, no red "you missed a day" warnings, no calorie or weight tracking
- **Calm, minimal interface** — designed to feel gentle and unobtrusive rather than clinical or naggy
- **Identity-first design** — framed around understanding yourself, not optimizing a metric

## 🎯 Design Philosophy

Lantern was built on a simple premise: health apps don't need to guilt-trip users into engagement to be effective. By removing streaks, punitive notifications, and diet-culture-adjacent metrics, Lantern aims to be a tool people actually *want* to open — because it feels supportive, not surveillant.
