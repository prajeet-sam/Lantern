# Privacy

Lantern is designed around one principle: **your cycle data belongs on your device —
and, if you opt into Sync, only a copy that nobody can read (not even us) ever leaves it.**

## What happens with your data

- **On-device first.** Everything you log is written to an on-device IndexedDB
  database. There is no analytics, and no third-party scripts.
- **Sync is opt-in.** Until you create an account (Settings → Sync), nothing is
  uploaded and there are no accounts.
- **When Sync is on, the server still can't read your data.** Your dataset is sealed
  with a key derived from your password (PBKDF2, 200,000 rounds, AES-256-GCM) *before*
  it leaves your device. Only salts and ciphertext are stored on the server.
- **The CSP `connect-src` allows only `'self'` plus your configured Supabase origin**,
  so accidental or silent exfiltration to any other third party is structurally
  impossible.
- **No tracking SDKs.** No ads, no fingerprinting.
- **Recovery key.** At sign-up you're shown a one-time recovery key. Only a SHA-256
  hash of it is stored, used solely to reset your password if you forget it. If you
  lose both your password and the key, your data cannot be recovered — by anyone.
- **Notifications** are generated by the browser on the same device (and only when you
  enable them). "Detailed" mode shows cycle wording on the OS lock screen by design —
  choose "Generic" if you don't want that.

## Encryption

- When you set a **privacy PIN** (Settings → Privacy lock), your logs and journal
  content are encrypted **at rest** with AES-256-GCM. The key is derived from your PIN
  with 200,000 rounds of PBKDF2 and is never stored; it only exists in memory while the
  app is unlocked.
- With a PIN set, someone who gets your device (or extracts the IndexedDB files)
  cannot read your cycle history without the PIN.
- Without a PIN, data is stored in plaintext on your device for convenience. **Setting
  a PIN is the recommended baseline** if you share your device or want protection at
  rest.
- Date keys and settings (e.g. theme, typical cycle length) remain plaintext because
  they are queryable; they reveal far less than the content they index.
- **Exports:** a plain JSON backup contains your data in readable form — keep it safe.
  Use **Export (encrypted backup)** for anything you store in a cloud folder; it is
  sealed with a password you choose and cannot be read without it.
- **Delete account** (Settings → Sync → Delete account) permanently deletes the
  account, the encrypted cloud copy, and the recovery-code hash. Local data stays.

## Observability without telemetry

- Lantern keeps a small, **bounded (200 events), content-free audit trail** on the
  device (unlocks, PIN changes, imports, wipes, sync events) so you can diagnose
  problems or spot tampering from Settings → Diagnostics. It contains no health data
  and is never transmitted.
- Errors are logged only to the browser console (visible to you alone), and the error
  screens show no personal data.

## Deletion

- **Delete everything** (Settings → Privacy & data) wipes the IndexedDB database, the
  vault, and the local audit trail from the device. If you're signed in to Sync, it
  also removes the encrypted copy from the server and signs you out, so nothing
  "resurrects" on the next sync.
- **Delete account** removes the cloud account entirely (see above).
- Browser-level data (e.g. `localStorage`) can be cleared in browser settings; the
  content-free audit trail, the Supabase session, and theme/reminder flags are the
  only `localStorage` items.

## Notes

- A service worker caches only the app shell (static assets); it never caches your
  cycle data.
- Lantern does not and cannot (by construction) sell, share, or derive revenue from
  your data.
